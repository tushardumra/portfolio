About Me

Hello! I'm Tushar, a passionate and creative web developer based in Ludhiana, India. My journey into the world of code began with a fascination for how elegant design and powerful technology could come together to create amazing digital experiences.

I just finished my MERN stack course, and now I'm focused on making projects that matter and getting ready for real-world problems. I want to be a part of a dynamic team, improve my skills as a software engineer, and turn ideas into useful, working digital experiences. When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring the outdoors, reading a good book, or working on personal projects that challenge me to think outside the box.
